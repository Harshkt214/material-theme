Raju ki 10 Galtiyan

RRaju har naya retail trader hai. Shayad tum bhi. Har galti ka asli naam, kaise pehchano, aur fix. Jo galti tumne bhi ki hai, uska dabba tick karo.
1
Loss mein SL door khiskana
Loss aversion
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOPrice SL ke paas aaye aur tum SL thoda aur door kar do, "bas thoda saans lene do".
FIXSL entry se pehle tay karo aur broker mein lagao. Loss mein SL kabhi door nahi.
Source: Tversky & Kahneman (1992): loss ka dard barabar jeet ki khushi se lagbhag do guna (ek estimate; naye studies kam batate hain).
2
Profit jaldi book, loss hold
Disposition effect
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOJournal mein average win chhota aur average loss bada. Profit mein darr, loss mein umeed.
FIXLast 10 trades ka average win vs average loss nikalo. Exit entry se pehle likho.
Source: Odean (1998): log profit wale shares jaldi bechte hain, loss wale pakad kar rakhte hain.
3
Averaging down
Loser mein add karna
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOPosition loss mein hai aur tum "average kam karne" ke liye aur kharid lete ho.
FIXRule likho: loss wali position mein kabhi add nahi. Add sirf profit wali mein, plan se.
Source: Tom Hougaard, Best Loser Wins (2022).
4
Zyada trade, zyada kharcha
Overconfidence · overtrading
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANODin ke trades ginti se bahar. Bina setup ke bhi click, "ek aur try".
FIXEk din ki max trade limit tay karo (jaise 3) aur use rules mein likho.
Source: Barber & Odean (2000), ek US study (1991-96): sabse zyada trade karne wale households ko 11.4% saalana mila, market ne 17.9% diya.
5
Sirf apne favour ke saboot
Confirmation bias
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOBullish ho to har chart bullish dikhta hai. Ulta signal "noise" lagta hai.
FIXHar trade se pehle ek reason likho jo trade ke KHILAAF ho.
Source: Daniel Kahneman, Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011).

Galti 6 se 10

6
Last 3 trades se system badalna
Recency bias
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANO3 loss ke baad perfect setup bhi skip, ya naya indicator dhoondna.
FIXAgle 20 trades ka sample tay karo. Beech mein system mat badlo.
Source: Kahneman (2011); Mark Douglas, Trading in the Zone (2000).
7
"Mujhe pata tha!"
Hindsight bias
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOChart ke baad sab obvious lagta hai, aur apni skill zyada lagti hai.
FIXTrade se PEHLE plan likho. Baad mein plan se compare karo, yaad se nahi.
Source: Kahneman (2011).
8
"Bas mera paisa wapas aa jaaye"
Loss mein risk lena · hope
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOBreak-even ka intezaar. Loss badhta hai par position band nahi hoti.
FIXHar open loss par poocho: "Ye trade aaj naya hota to leta?" Nahi = band karo.
Source: Kahneman (2011); Hougaard (2022).
9
Bhaagti candle ke peeche bhaagna
FOMO
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOBreakout nikal gaya, tum top par kood gaye. Late entry, bada SL.
FIXRule: candle bhaag gayi to chase nahi. Pullback ka wait, ya agla setup.
Source: Jared Tendler, The Mental Game of Trading (2021).
10
Market se badla
Revenge trading
maine bhi ki
KAISE PEHCHANOLoss ke turant baad naya trade, aksar bade size mein, gusse mein.
FIXHar loss ke baad 15 minute screen band. Daily max loss pe din khatam.
Source: Tendler (2021).
