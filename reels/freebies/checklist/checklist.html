Pre-Trade Checklist

Order lagane se pehle 10 sawaal. Har sawaal ka jawab Haan ya Nahi. Phone par screenshot rakho, ya print karke screen ke paas.
SETUP
1
Ye mera likha hua setup hai?
Rules wali list mein hai, ya bas "accha lag raha hai"?
HAANNAHI
2
Entry ka reason ek line mein likh sakta hoon?
Nahi likh pa rahe = reason nahi hai.
HAANNAHI
3
News check ki? Trade ke beech koi bada event nahi?
Result, policy, data release: pehle calendar dekho.
HAANNAHI
RISK
4
Stop loss tay hai, aur broker mein lagaunga?
SL dimaag mein nahi, order mein.
HAANNAHI
5
Is trade ka risk ₹ mein pata hai, aur limit ke andar hai?
Quantity SL ke hisaab se, mood ke hisaab se nahi.
HAANNAHI
6
SL lag gaya to bhi theek hai? Risk accept hai?
Mann mein bolo: "Ye ₹___ jaa sakte hain, theek hai."
HAANNAHI
7
Aaj ki daily loss limit aur max trades abhi baaki hain?
Limit poori = aaj ka din khatam.
HAANNAHI
EXIT + MANN
8
Exit plan pehle se likha hai? (target ya exit ka rule)
Target aaye to kya karoge, abhi tay karo.
HAANNAHI
9
Main shaant hoon? Gussa, FOMO, boredom nahi?
Pichhle loss ka badla ya chhoota hua move pakadna to nahi?
HAANNAHI
10
Trade galat gaya to bhi rules par tikunga?
SL nahi khiskaunga, loss mein add nahi karunga.
HAANNAHI
Ek bhi "Nahi" = trade nahi.

Setup dobara aayega. Market kahin nahi ja raha. Trade ke baad journal mein likho: checklist poori thi ya nahi.

Base: Mark Douglas, Trading in the Zone (2000): edge define karo, risk pehle tay karo, risk accept karo. Sawaal apne shabdon mein.
