Trading in the Zone: 5 Truths

Mark Douglas ki book ke 5 sach aur 7 principles, apne shabdon mein. Roz market se pehle ek baar padho.
1

Market mein kuch bhi ho sakta hai.

Kisi bhi pal koi bhi cheez price ko hila sakti hai, jo tumhe dikhti bhi nahi.

Trading mein: perfect setup par bhi SL lag sakta hai. Isliye har trade ka SL pehle se.
2

Paisa banane ke liye ye jaanna zaroori nahi ki aage kya hoga.

Next candle kisi ko nahi pata. Kaam hai plan follow karna, prediction nahi.

Trading mein: "pata nahi" maan kar bhi entry, SL aur exit likh sakte ho.
3

Ek edge par wins aur losses kis order mein aayenge, ye random hai.

Achha setup bhi lagatar 3 loss de sakta hai. Ek trade ka result kuch saabit nahi karta.

Trading mein: setup ko ek trade se nahi, bahut saare trades (jaise 20) ke baad aanko.
4

Edge sirf ek probability hai, guarantee nahi.

Edge batata hai ki ek result doosre se zyada hone ke chance hain. Bas itna.

Trading mein: casino har haath nahi jeetta. Tumhe bhi har trade jeetne ki zaroorat nahi.
5

Market ka har pal naya hai.

Aaj ka chart pichhle hafte jaisa dikh sakta hai, par wo wahi pal nahi hai.

Trading mein: pichhle loss ka darr ya pichhle win ka josh agle trade mein mat le jao.

Consistency ke 7 principles

1Main apna edge saaf, objective tareeke se define karta hoon.
2Har trade ka risk entry se pehle tay karta hoon.
3Risk ko poora accept karta hoon, warna trade nahi leta.
4Edge dikhe to bina hichak, bina sawaal action leta hoon.
5Market jab profit de, main profit leta hoon.
6Apni galtiyon par lagatar nazar rakhta hoon.
7Main in principles ki zaroorat samajhta hoon, isliye inhe kabhi nahi todta.
Source: Trading in the Zone, Mark Douglas (2000). Ye summary apne shabdon mein hai, book ka text nahi. Poori samajh ke liye asli book padho.
