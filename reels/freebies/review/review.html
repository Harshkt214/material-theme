C-game ko upar laao

Kyun ye review?
Jared Tendler (The Mental Game of Trading) ka "Inchworm" idea: tumhari trading ek range mein hoti hai, best (A-game) se worst (C-game) tak. Sabse tez sudhaar C-game ki galtiyan kam karne se aata hai, best din ko aur best banane se nahi.

Aur Annie Duke (Thinking in Bets): result se decision mat aanko. Achha decision bhi loss de sakta hai. Isliye sawaal decisions par hain, P&L par nahi.
